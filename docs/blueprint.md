# **App Name**: Khalil Premium

## Core Features:

- Product Grid Display: Showcase a responsive grid of active products, emphasizing one card per model, with dynamic banners for promotions and quick filters for storage, condition, and price.
- Detailed Product View: Display zoomable product images and a gallery, offering dynamic pricing based on storage selection and detailed information on condition, warranty, and delivery.
- Interactive Shopping Cart: Provide a side drawer cart for easy addition, deletion, and quantity adjustment of products, with a dynamically updated total price and integrated checkout process.
- Streamlined Checkout Process: Enable customer form completion for name, phone, and address during checkout, along with a delivery mode selector and admin-activated payment methods.
- AI-assisted Exchange Request: Implement an AI tool in the exchange request process, collecting details like the model to be exchanged, photo uploads, desired model, and contact phone for fast estimate of value. An admin user will finalize the valuation

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#22315F) to convey premium quality and trustworthiness.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F2F5) to create a clean, modern canvas.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) to highlight special offers and premium selections, reflecting luxury.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' sans-serif for a contemporary, precise feel, ideal for short text and headlines.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' humanist sans-serif combines modernity with warmth and personality; suitable for both headlines and body text. Recommendation: pair 'Poppins' for headlines with 'PT Sans' for longer blocks of text
- Use sleek, minimalist icons reflecting Apple product aesthetics.
- Maintain a grid-based layout for product listings and ensure responsive design for optimal viewing on all devices.
